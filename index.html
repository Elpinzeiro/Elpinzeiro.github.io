About me

I am a PhD researcher (CIFRE industrial PhD) at EURECOM and the Huawei Paris Research Center, supervised by Pietro Michiardi (EURECOM) and Zied Ben Houidi (Huawei). My research direction is Scalable Update of Large Language Models with Episodic Memories for Continuous Knowledge Integration.

I work on continual learning in large language models: adding new knowledge to a model's weights so that the model can use it as flexibly as knowledge learned in pretraining, without damaging what it already knows. I diagnose where these updates fail by combining two views: large-scale tests of frontier models on real facts and controlled fine-tuning experiments on synthetic data. I try to take inspiration from how our brain solves these problems, and then re-apply those principles to LLMs.

I am first author of an accepted NeurIPS 2026 paper on episodic binding in parametric memory, and co-author of a second one on surprise and catastrophic forgetting. I am also first author of a UniReps 2026 workshop paper (extended abstract) on how supervision changes the depth at which injected facts become readable inside LLMs. See my publications.

Before my PhD, I spent three years as a data scientist at Rulex in Genoa. I hold an M.Sc. in Computer Engineering (110/110 cum laude) and a B.Sc. in Computer Engineering from the University of Genoa.

Research interests

  • Continual learning and knowledge injection in LLMs
  • Parametric and episodic memory
  • Catastrophic forgetting under fine-tuning (SFT, LoRA)
  • Mechanistic interpretability (logit lens, Jacobian lens)
  • Benchmark design and LLM-as-judge evaluation
